What makes a click

Six rules. Every thumbnail, every time.

01

One clear focus

One subject. The eye knows where to land.

02

Face and emotion

A real reaction sets the mood.

03

Three words or fewer

Adds to the title, never repeats it.

04

Contrast that stands out

Pops on light and dark YouTube.

05

Checked at phone size

If it reads small, it reads everywhere.

06

Matched to the title

One story, so the click feels worth it.

FAQ

Questions before you start

🚀Getting started

Do I need to get on a call?

No. You fill a 2-minute form and I send the scope, timeline and price within 24 hours. A call happens only if you ask for one.

How do I pay?

100% upfront, by PayPal or Wise. Monthly plans are paid before each month starts. First I send you the scope in writing, and you reply "Yes" before you pay.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. Agencies get white-label rates at 20% off the prices on this page. Tell me about your clients in the form.

Do you work with company pages too?

Yes. I run both personal profiles and company pages, including the Keys Media Consulting company page.

💼LinkedIn posts

What's the difference between Consistent and Full Feed?

Both include strategy, a monthly plan, writing in your voice with 3 captions to choose from on every post, design, publishing and a monthly report. Consistent ($249/month) is 3 posts a week. Full Feed ($449/month) is 5 posts a week. Commit to 3 months on either and your Profile Setup is free.

How do I approve posts?

Every Friday you get a folder with next week's posts. Reply in one message with your choices or changes. If you don't reply in 48 hours, my recommended caption goes live.

Will the posts sound like me?

Yes. Before the first post I study your past posts, your offer and how you talk. You also get 3 captions on every post, so you pick the one that sounds most like you.

Do you reply to comments for me?

No. Replies should come from you, so your audience talks to the real person. I handle planning, writing, design and publishing.

🪪Profile Setup

Do you write my headline and About too?

Yes. Profile Setup includes your positioning line, 3 headline options and a full About section, written from a short questionnaire. You approve the text before any design starts.

What do you need from me for Profile Setup?

Answers to 8 short questions, your best photo, your brand colours or website, and a screenshot of your LinkedIn analytics so we can compare results after 30 days.

🎨Design Pack

I already write my posts. Can you just design them?

Yes, that's the Design Pack. Send your text any time and get the designs in 3 working days.

Can I change the post types in my Design Pack?

Yes, you can swap one type for another. The only swap not allowed is an image post for a carousel.

🎯YouTube thumbnails

How long does a thumbnail take?

48 hours from the brief (24 hours on monthly packs), with 2 revisions.

What is ABC testing?

YouTube's Test & Compare shows 3 thumbnails to different viewers and keeps the one that gets the most watch time. I design all 3 so you test real ideas, not colour changes.
