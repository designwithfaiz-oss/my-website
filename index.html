What makes a click

Six rules. Every thumbnail, every time.

01

One clear focus

One main subject, so the eye knows where to land in a split second.

02

Face and emotion

A real reaction tells the viewer how the video will feel before they click.

03

Three words or fewer

The text adds to the title. It never repeats it.

04

Contrast that stands out

Colours and shapes that pop on both light and dark YouTube.

05

Checked at phone size

I check every thumbnail small first. If it reads on a phone, it reads everywhere.

06

Matched to the title

Thumbnail and title tell one story together, so the click feels worth it.
